Chai pe Charcha
☕ Chai pe Charcha

Five friends. One cup of chai. Only one of them is right. Three groups, nine rounds. Look closely, then tap the friend whose idea you trust!

How to play
  1. 3 groups, 9 rounds. Five friends sit around the table. Each one gives an idea in a bubble. Only one idea is right.
  2. Look closely. The clue picture and the question are at the top. Read them, then check every bubble.
  3. Tap a friend to pick that idea, then press Lock answer.
  4. Turns: the first group scores 1 point. If it is wrong, that friend is crossed out and the next group can steal 1 point. The last group scores 2 points. The starting group changes every round.
  5. Time: each turn starts with 2:00 and the clock shrinks every round down to 0:45. Pass gives the turn away. Pause stops the clock and hides the ideas.
  6. Levels: the more the leading group scores, the harder the next questions become.
  7. After the answer, the green bubble shows the right idea and why it is right.

Practice shows a random question with the ideas shuffled. No points are counted.
