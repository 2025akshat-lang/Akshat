Measurement of Length & Motion
1. Non-Standard Units (गैर-मानक मात्रक)▶

Measurement by body (शरीर के अंगों से मापन)

  • Foot steps — for short lengths (पैर के कदम — छोटी लंबाई के लिए)
  • Arm length — for longer lengths (हाथ — लंबी लंबाई के लिए)
  • Hand span(Balisht) (बालिश्त) — distance from thumb tip to little-finger tip with fingers stretched out

Ancient units

Angul, Dhanush, Yojan (अंगुल, धनुष, योजन)

Why not reliable?

These units are not exact — they change from person to person, so they are not accepted as standard. ये मात्रक Exact नहीं होते, हर व्यक्ति के अनुसार बदलते हैं, इसलिए Standard नहीं माने जाते।

2. Standard Units (मानक मात्रक)▶

What is it?

A form of measuring length that is exact and equal for everyone — this is the S.I. Unit of length. Length मापने का ऐसा रूप जो सबके लिए Equal / Exact हो — S.I. Unit of Length कहलाता है।

Conversions to remember

1 kilometre = 1000 metres

1 metre = 1/1000 kilometres

1 metre = 100 centimetres

1 centimetre = 1/100 metres

1 centimetre = 10 millimetres

1millimetres = 1/10 centimetre

1 metre = 1000 millimetres

3. Position, Reference Point & Motion▶

Position (स्थिति)

The exact address of an object measured from a fixed point.

Reference Point (संदर्भ बिंदु)

A fixed point from which the distance and direction of an object is described. Motion is measured starting from here.

Motion vs Rest

  • Motion — change in position with respect to a reference point over time
  • Rest — no change in position

Types of Motion

Linear Motion (सरल रेखीय गति)

Motion in a straight line. Example: a bus, a metro train, kids marching in a line.

Circular Motion (वृत्तीय गति)

An object moves around a fixed centre point. Example: a fan blade, a merry-go-round / carousel.

Oscillatory Motion (दोलन गति)

An object moves back and forth repeatedly along the same path. Example: a swing, a pendulum.

4. Correct Way of Measurement (सही मापन कैसे करें?)▶

Rules for accurate measurement

  1. Place the object parallel to the scale.
  2. Keep your eye directly above the scale to avoid a parallax error.
  3. If the scale has a broken start, use: Length = Final point − Initial point
  4. To measure a curved line, use a thread along the curve, then measure the thread on the scale.

Measuring with a broken scale (टूटे हुए स्केल से मापन)

Remember

Even with a broken scale, always use final point − initial point to get the correct measurement.
