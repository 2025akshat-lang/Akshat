Lesson Plan: Earth as a System

1. Preliminary Information

Subject: Integrated Science
Class: IX-D
Topic: Chapter 13 - Earth as a System
Duration: 40 Minutes
Sub-Topic: 5 Spheres Interconnectedness & Albedo
No. of Learners: 40
Medium: Hindi and English

2. Specific Learning Objectives

  • Knowledge: Students will be able to list and define the 5 spheres of the Earth (Geosphere, Hydrosphere, Cryosphere, Atmosphere, Biosphere).
  • Understanding: Students will explain how solar radiation heats the Earth's surface and define terms like Insolation and Albedo Effect.
  • Application & Analysis: Students will analyze how a disturbance in one sphere (e.g., melting of glaciers in Cryosphere) impacts other spheres (Hydrosphere, Biosphere).

3. Teaching / Instructional Aids

  • Chalkboard and Colored Chalks
  • Activity Chart showing 5 Spheres Interconnectedness
  • Demonstration items for Albedo: Black paper/soil vs. White sheet/ice surface
  • Diagram of Electromagnetic Spectrum

5. Presentation (Teaching-Learning Process)

Teaching Point Teacher Activity (Q&A & Explanation) Student Activity Blackboard Work
1. The 5 Interconnected Spheres Defines 5 Spheres: Geosphere, Hydrosphere, Cryosphere, Atmosphere, Biosphere.

Discussion: "How does less winter snowfall affect sheep grazing grass in summer?" 		Students trace the link: Less snow → Low lake water → Dried grass → Less food for sheep. Sphere Web:
Cryosphere → Hydrosphere → Biosphere
2. Solar Radiation & Insolation Q: "Is Sun's energy equal everywhere?"

Explanation: Defines Insolation and Solar Constant (~1.4 kW/m²). Highlights Anna Mani's contribution in mapping India's solar potential. 		Students note key definitions and calculate solar energy values (3.6 × 10⁶ J/m²/hr). • Solar Constant: 1.4 kW/m²
• Pioneer: Anna Mani (1952)
3. Reflection & Albedo Effect Concept: High albedo reflects heat (Snow: 0.8-0.9), Low albedo absorbs heat (Black soil/water).

Question: "Why are cities warmer than villages at night?" 		Students identify Urban Heat Island Effect: Concrete roads absorb heat during the day and re-radiate at night. Albedo Scale:
Snow/Ice → Cool (High Reflection)
Black Soil/Asphalt → Hot (High Absorption)

6. Recapitulation & Home Assignments

Recapitulation Questions:

  • Name the 5 spheres of Earth with one example each.
  • Why does snow remain cool while black roads get extremely hot in summer?
  • Define Insolation.

Home Assignments (From Log Book Page 79):

  • Write your thought on: What will happen if there is no differential heating of the Earth?
  • Draw a neat labelled diagram of the various layers of atmosphere (Troposphere to Exosphere).
  • Sketch the Electromagnetic Spectrum highlighting High Frequency (Short Wavelength) vs Lower Frequency (Long Wavelength) rays.
