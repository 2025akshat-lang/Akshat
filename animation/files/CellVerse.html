Prokaryote vs Eukaryote
FeatureProkaryote (bacteria)Eukaryote (animal)
Size0.1 - 5 µm10 - 100 µm
NucleusNahi hai; DNA nucleoid region mein, koi membrane nahiTrue nucleus, double nuclear envelope + pores
DNASingle circular chromosome + plasmids; histones nahi (HU proteins)Multiple linear chromosomes wrapped on histones; mtDNA bhi
Ribosomes70S (50S + 30S), free in cytoplasm80S (60S + 40S), free + bound to RER; mitochondria mein 70S
Membrane organellesKoi nahi (mesosome artefact hai)ER, Golgi, mitochondria, lysosome, peroxisome
Cell wallPeptidoglycan (bacteria)Animal cell mein nahi; plant: cellulose, fungi: chitin
CytoskeletonSimple homologs: FtsZ, MreBMicrotubules, actin, intermediate filaments
FlagellumRotating flagellin motor (proton-driven), membrane se bahar9+2 microtubule axoneme, whip-like, membrane se ghira
EnergyPlasma membrane par ETCMitochondria (cristae) mein ETC
Gene expressionTranscription + translation saath-saath (coupled)Nucleus mein transcription, cytoplasm mein translation
DivisionBinary fissionMitosis / meiosis (spindle, centrosome)
Origin~3.5 billion saal pehle~1.5-2 billion saal pehle (endosymbiosis)
ExamplesE. coli, Bacillus, cyanobacteria, archaeaAnimals, plants, fungi, protists
